About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: create a welcoming neighborhood bakery where fresh food and community come together.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose dependable ingredients and favor seasonal and locally available products when practical. Small-batch preparation helps us focus on freshness and reduce unnecessary waste.

Staff Highlights

Lead Baker

Our lead baker oversees breads and fermentation and works with the team to maintain consistent quality.

Pastry Team

Our pastry team prepares daily favorites and develops seasonal items for the bakery case.

Customer Team

Our front-of-house team helps customers with product questions, pre-orders, and celebration planning.
